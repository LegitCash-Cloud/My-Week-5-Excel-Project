### Description
As part of the ongoing efforts to optimize sales performance, improve menu offerings, and enhance customer experience, this food outlet requires a structured analysis of their Pizza Sales Data (2015). The dataset contains transactional details, including order volume, pricing, pizza categories, and timestamps.

![Pizza Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5a4ea4d8-32c2-4187-8adc-de2421690558)

### Aim Of This Project
The primary objective of this project are as follows:
- Identify high-performing and underperforming menu items.
- Understand peak sales periods to optimize staffing and resource allocation.
- Assess pricing strategies and their impact on revenue and sales volume.
- Improve customer experience by aligning menu offerings with demand patterns.
- Enhance inventory planning by analyzing ingredient usage and wastage trends.
### Insights And Recommendations
1. Total Revenue Performance

- Total Revenue over the period was consistently high, indicating strong market demand.

- Revenue peaked in July, suggesting a seasonal trend or successful promotion during that month. Consider launching similar promos again.

---

2. Monthly Sales Trends

- Sales Volume shows a steady upward trend from January to July, then a slight dip towards the end of the year.

- This seasonal pattern can guide inventory planning and marketing budgets, focusing heavily between Q2 and Q3.

---

3. Sales by Pizza Category

- Classic and Supreme pizzas dominate in revenue, making them the top selling categories.

- The Veggie category lags behind either due to lower demand or insufficient promotion. It’s a good idea to run limited time offers or combo deals to improve its performance.

---

4. Best & Worst Selling Pizzas

- Top Performing:

The classic deluxe pizza is the top contributor to both revenue and quantity sold.

- Low Performing:

The brie carre pizza have minimal sales and may need:

  - Product redesign

  - Better placement on the menu

  - Customer feedback survey

---

5. Sales by Pizza Size

- L sized pizza have the highest sales volume, suggesting they are the most popular choice.

---

6. Hourly Sales Performance

- Sales are strongest during lunch (12–2 PM) and evening (6–8 PM) time blocks.

This insight is useful for:

  - Staff scheduling

  - Targeted advertising on food delivery apps during peak hours

  - Planning promotions during off-peak hours to boost slow periods

---

7. Weekly Sales Trends

- Friday and Saturday are peak sales days, showing high consumer activity before and during the weekend.

- Consider running weekly “Pizza Friday” campaigns to capitalize on this natural spike.

---

8. Average Order Size

- The average customer buys ~2 pizzas per order, which is decent.

- Offering “Buy 2, Get 1 Free” deals or family meal bundles could raise the order size and total basket value.
